## Damages and Issues

Inspect orders immediately upon arrival. Contact us without delay if any
item is defective, damaged, or incorrect so we can resolve the matter.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned:

- Prescription medications, including Spicy Alien Hot Sauce, once dispensed
  (subject to state board-of-pharmacy rules)
- Perishable goods
- Custom or personalized products
- Personal care goods once opened
- Hazardous materials or temperature-sensitive shipments
- Sale items or gift cards

## Exchanges

For non-prescription items, return your current item first. Once the
return is accepted, purchase the replacement separately for the fastest
service.

Prescription exchanges (e.g., dosage adjustments) are handled through your
prescribing clinician inside the patient portal, not via this refund
process.

## Refunds

Upon receipt and inspection of an approved return, we will notify you of
the refund decision. Approved refunds are processed to your original
payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may add
additional processing time. Contact us if 15 or more business days have
passed since approval and you have not received the refund.
